Brand

KIMMOO

KIMMOO is a portable-cooling brand with a focused line of rechargeable pocket hand warmers. The current catalog has 18 fan lines and 3 hand-warmer lines, sold in the United States on Amazon and TikTok Shop.

KIMMOO Amazon store

Fans include handheld, desk, clip-on, stroller, waist, and camping models. Hand warmers include two cute-bear two-packs and an embossed floral pocket warmer that appeared in the official KIMMOO brand search (the listing title does not prepend KIMMOO). Browse by category: Fans and Hand warmers.

Product photography can be added later. The tiles on this page are original typographic placeholders, not Amazon images, and are shown only to distinguish each product line.

This website does not publish official list prices. Current pricing is set by Amazon and may change. Use the Shop on Amazon links to see live offers.
