About

A consumer-electronics company built around portable comfort.

極客創新有限公司 develops and sells everyday personal devices for cooling and warmth. Our products are offered in the United States through Amazon and TikTok Shop under the KIMMOO and DIGIX brands.

What we sell

Portable fans, rechargeable hand warmers, food warming mats, a handheld garment steamer, and a fireplace-style humidifier offered in the DIGIX store.

Where we sell

In the United States, on Amazon storefronts for KIMMOO and DIGIX, and on TikTok Shop product pages.

How to reach us

Business inquiries may be sent to contact@geek-innovation.com. We do not list a public phone number here.

Who we are

極客創新有限公司 is the company behind KIMMOO and DIGIX. This website is the official brand site for those products. It is an informational and marketing site: it does not include a shopping cart or checkout.

We keep this page factual. We have not published a founding story, executive biographies, awards, or a registered office address here because those details have not been confirmed for this site. The registered office will be added once it is confirmed.

The brands

KIMMOO is a portable-fan brand with a smaller line of rechargeable pocket hand warmers. The current catalog includes handheld, desk, clip-on, stroller, waist, and camping fans, plus three hand-warmer lines.

DIGIX covers personal fans and rechargeable magnetic hand warmers, and also includes home and kitchen products: three food-warming-mat lines, a 2-in-1 steamer and iron, and a fireplace-style ultrasonic humidifier / essential oil diffuser offered through the official DIGIX Amazon store and brand search.

How products are sold

Customers purchase KIMMOO and DIGIX products on Amazon and TikTok Shop. Those platforms set live prices, shipping, returns, and order support. Links on this site point to official storefronts or to known product pages. This website does not process payments.
